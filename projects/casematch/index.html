Casematch

A hiring platform built only for special education and ABA jobs.

Visit joincasematch.com (opens in a new tab) Back to projects

Why I'm building it

General job boards don't understand these roles. Credentials, settings, and the kind of work involved all matter in special education and ABA, and a keyword search on a general board misses most of that.

So Casematch is made for this field and nothing else. I design and build it myself, and I work with BCBAs to test and verify it. It's in development now.
